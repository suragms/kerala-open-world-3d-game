# Phase 10 visual references and fidelity

The geographic datasets, coordinates and source footprints are retained. No Level A reconstruction is claimed. Imported footprints support Level B geography; the roof, facade, vegetation, station layouts and landmark detail authored in this phase remain approximate Level C art.

| Landmark | Official reference | Detail informed by the reference | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vadakkunnathan, Thrissur | [Incredible India, Ministry of Tourism](https://www.prod.incredibleindia.gov.in/content/incredible-india-v2/en/destinations/thrissur/vadakkumnathan-temple.html), [Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/destination/vadakkumnatha-temple-thrissur/70/) | Four cardinal gateway silhouettes, timber and tiled roof vocabulary | Small exterior composition; dimensions, carvings and precinct layout are not surveyed |
| Palakkad Fort | [Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/malabar/palakkad-fort/22), [Palakkad district government](https://palakkad.nic.in/en/tourist-place/palakkad-fort/) | Granite colour, moat enclosure and entrance bridge | Procedural moat and bridge; historic plan and masonry are not reconstructed |
| Bekal Fort | [Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/campaigns/kerala365/bekal-fort-kasaragod-history), [Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/kerala/bekal/bekal-fort) | Laterite colour, observation tower and broad steps | Original approximate silhouette; no exact fort plan or terrain survey |

References were consulted on 1 November 2026. They guide selected architectural features, not a claim of photographic matching. No downloaded reference photographs or proprietary operator liveries are redistributed.

The fourteen district profiles in `src/game/regional.ts` implement the landscape art direction requested in the Phase 10 brief. Actual sourced crop/tree tags override the profile where available. Procedural tree placement, colours and species distributions do not establish real plantation boundaries. Streets, water, building footprints and terrain continue to use the imported geographic data.

Mapped bus terminals retain their source locations and explicit Level C labels. Added bay, waiting-line and circulation markings belong to the approximate facility model; they are not newly mapped roads or a verified terminal plan.
